The problem

Where training follow-up gets lost

Expiry dates

Live in the training system or a spreadsheet. The crew member learns about an expiring check from an email, or from a roster change.

Notices

Go out by email or a crew portal. Proving who read which one before a duty takes a day of chasing.

Courses

Are assigned in one place, completed in another and confirmed by hand.

With Crewops Academy

The crew member opens My training, sees what is next and when each check expires, and takes the course and its exam there. The department sees the gaps across its crews on one page, with signatures and results dated.

On the crew phone

What your crews see

Inside the crew app your crews already use for their roster, in seven languages. My training sits on Me, under My airline.

My training

One screen on Me, under My airline: what is up next, how many items are pending, and the days left on each, in colour as the date gets close. Each crew member also sees their own stats.

Read and understood

Crew notices, safety bulletins, safety memos, coordination sheets, security notices, training documents and revisions, each with its reading time and due date. One tap signs it, with the date.

My qualifications

Each check and course on record, with the date it was done and the date it expires, as the training department holds them.

Courses

A course is a set of lessons: video, PDF or a link. Crews download the lessons and study them with no signal. A required course has a due date; a self-study course waits until the crew member wants it.

Exams and certificates

The exam is graded on the server, so the answers never reach the phone and an exam needs a connection. Your pass mark decides the result, the portal keeps the latest attempt and the attempt count, and a pass puts the certificate on the phone.

SCORM modules

The e-learning modules you already have play inside the app, offline. The module's own result (passed, completed or failed, with its score) completes the course, and the bookmark keeps the crew member's place between sessions.

Offline

Downloaded lessons and SCORM modules open with no signal, and so does the last feed the phone loaded. Signatures made offline wait and go out when the phone is back online. Exams are graded on the server, so they need a connection.

One phone per crew code

The first phone to open a crew code is paired with it; other phones are refused until the department resets it. A code nobody has opened yet belongs to whoever opens it first.

In the operator portal

What your department does

In its own panel of the operator portal (hawkmason.com/crewops/portal/academy), signed in with your airline's operator code; a code can open this product alone. Nothing to install.

Today board

Training joins the portal's Today board with the other products you hold: qualifications expired or expiring within 30 days, required qualifications missing for a role, and notices and required courses past their date that not everyone has done. Each line opens its record.

Qualification matrix

One row per crew member, one column per qualification. Each cell shows expired, missing, expiring within 30 days or valid. Filter flight or cabin crew and see the gaps counted.

Role requirements

Set which qualifications each role needs, and the matrix shows who is missing one. The defaults: flight crew OPC, LPC, line check, SEP, CRM, DG, medical and licence; cabin crew recurrent, SEP, first aid, CRM, DG and medical.

Records from your system

Upload a CSV exported from your training system: crew code, role, name, item, issued, expires. Each crew member's record is replaced by the new rows, so the phones show what your system holds. The API imports records from your HR or training system.

Notices

Publish a notice to everyone, to flight crew or cabin crew, or to a list of crew codes, with a due date and reading time. See who has signed and who has not.

Courses and exams

Build a course from video (MP4), PDF and link lessons uploaded to Crewops, and add an exam with its pass mark. Make it required with a due date, or leave it as self-study. Results and the attempt count are kept.

SCORM import

Upload a SCORM 1.2 or 2004 package from the portal and it becomes a course. Your crews play it in the app, offline, and the module's own result completes the course.

Export

The records on file as CSV, for your training system, your audit file or your authority.

Connections

Fits your systems

Crewops Academy works on its own. When your department already runs a training system, it connects to it instead of standing beside it.

On its own

Load records by CSV, build courses and notices in the portal and follow the department from there. Nothing to integrate.

Records in

By CSV upload, or through the API with a key made for your HR or training system. You make and revoke the key in the portal.

Webhooks out

When a crew member signs a notice or completes a course, a signed message goes to the https endpoint you set, so your training system can record it. Each event is sent once, and the portal lists the last 20 deliveries.

Your e-learning

SCORM 1.2 and 2004 packages come in as they are and play offline on the crew phone.

API reference

Every endpoint is documented at hawkmason.com/crewops/api.

Regulatory basis

Built on the rules you work to

The default qualification types are the ones the crew rules set, and expiry dates come from your records. Crewops Academy is built around those rules; your procedures and records prevail.

  • ORO.MLR.115: record keeping: training, checking and qualification records kept for the periods the rule sets
  • ORO.FC.230: flight crew recurrent training and checking: operator proficiency check and line check validity
  • ORO.CC.140: cabin crew recurrent training
  • ORO.GEN.200: communicating safety information to the people who need it

Questions

What departments ask first

Does it replace our training management system?

No. It runs beside your training system or feeds it through the API, webhooks and CSV. Crews get notices, courses, exams and records on the phone. Your training records and their approvals stay in your system.

Is e-learning included?

Yes. Courses carry video and PDF lessons that crews download and study offline. SCORM 1.2 and 2004 packages play offline in the app, and a module's own result completes the course. Exams are graded on the server against your pass mark, so they need a connection.

Can crews see the exam answers?

No. The exam is graded on the server and the answers never reach the phone. The portal keeps each crew member's latest attempt and the attempt count, and a pass puts the certificate on the phone.

Which qualifications does the matrix check?

The ones you set for each role. By default, flight crew need OPC, LPC, line check, SEP, CRM, DG, medical and licence, and cabin crew need recurrent, SEP, first aid, CRM, DG and medical. You can change both lists in the portal.

Can flight crew and cabin crew get different notices?

Yes. A notice or course goes to everyone, to flight crew, to cabin crew, or to a list of crew codes.

What do our crews need?

The crew app, your airline's company code and their own crew code. No account and no password. Crews of an airline that has not bought Crewops Academy do not see My training.
