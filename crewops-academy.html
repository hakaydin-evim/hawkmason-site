The problem

Where training follow-up gets lost

Expiry dates

Live in the training system or a spreadsheet. The crew member learns about an expiring check from an email, or from a roster change.

Notices

Go out by email or a crew portal. Proving who read which one before a duty takes a day of chasing.

Courses

Are assigned in one place, completed in another and confirmed by hand.

With Crewops Academy

The crew member opens My Training and sees what is next and when it expires. The department sees the gaps across the fleet on one page, and every signature with its date.

On the crew phone

What your crews see

Inside the Crewops Crew app your crews already use for their roster, in seven languages.

My Training

One screen on Me: what is up next, how many items are pending, and the days left on each, in colour as the date gets close.

Read and understood

Crew notices, safety bulletins, safety memos, coordination sheets, security notices, training documents and revisions, each with its reading time and due date. One tap signs it, with the date.

My qualifications

Each check and course on record, with the date it was done and the date it expires, as the training department holds them.

Courses

An assigned course opens its document or link. "I completed it" sends the completion, and the training department verifies it.

Offline

The feed stays on the phone and reads with no signal. Signatures made offline wait and go out when the phone is back online.

Regulatory basis

Built on the rules you work to

The qualification types and their validity periods are the ones the crew rules already set. Crewops Academy supports your compliance; your procedures and records remain the reference.

  • ORO.MLR.115: record keeping: training, checking and qualification records kept for the periods the rule sets
  • ORO.FC.230: flight crew recurrent training and checking: operator proficiency check and line check validity
  • ORO.CC.140: cabin crew recurrent training
  • ORO.GEN.200: communicating safety information to the people who need it

Questions

What departments ask first

Does it replace our training management system?

No. Your training system stays the record of truth. Crewops Academy carries its records to the crew phones by CSV and brings back signatures and completions. An integration that writes back directly is built when your system allows it.

Is e-learning included?

A course is a link to your existing material or a document. Course playback with tracking inside the app is not in the first version.

Can flight crew and cabin crew get different notices?

Yes. A notice or course goes to everyone, to flight crew, to cabin crew, or to a list of crew codes.

What do our crews need?

The Crewops Crew app, your airline's code and their own crew code. No account and no password. Crews of an airline that has not bought Crewops Academy do not see My Training.
