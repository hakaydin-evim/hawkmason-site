Questions

What operators ask first

Can we buy one product?

Yes. Each product has its own licence, so you can hold one, two, three or all four. An operator code opens only the products it covers.

Where does the data live?

Roster, sleep and health entries stay on the phone. What a crew member files or signs (a report, a confirmation, a course result, a shared rating) is stored on Cloudflare servers that Hawk & Mason Studios runs, with each airline's records kept apart by its airline code, and read on the airline's side only with your operator code or API keys.

Do our crews need an account?

No. In the pilot programme, crews open the crew app with your airline's company code. They create no account and buy nothing in the app.

Does it work with the systems we already run?

Yes. Each product runs on its own beside your SMS, HR, training and document systems, or feeds them through the API, webhooks and exports.

Can you build a module we do not see here?

Write to us with the problem. We look at your files and rules first, then answer with a plan. A module appears here only once it is built.

Product names belong to their owners; Crewops has no affiliation with them. Crewops Insight supports a fatigue risk assessment and makes no judgement about fitness to fly. Your procedures and records prevail.
