Your airline, on a computer

Notices, training, documents and tasks from your airline, the same as in the app. What you confirm here counts as confirmed on your phone.

  1. Open Crewops Crew on your phone.
  2. Me → MY AIRLINE → Open on a computer.
  3. Type the 8-character code here. It works once, for ten minutes.

You stay signed in on this browser for 30 days, or until you sign out or end web sessions from your phone. On a shared computer, sign out when you are done.
