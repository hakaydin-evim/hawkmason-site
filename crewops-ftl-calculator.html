How the maximum FDP is set

Under ORO.FTL.205(b) the basic maximum daily FDP depends on two things: the local time you report, in the time zone you are acclimatised to, and how many sectors you fly. The best case is 13 hours for one or two sectors reporting between 06:00 and 13:29. Each sector from the third takes 30 minutes off, down to a floor of 9 hours. Afternoon and evening reports shorten the day in 15-minute steps; reports from 17:00 to 04:59 start at 11 hours.

If you are in an unknown state of acclimatisation, a separate table applies. It starts at 11 hours whatever the report time.

Minimum rest

ORO.FTL.235 sets the minimum rest before an FDP. At home base it is at least as long as the preceding duty period, or 12 hours, whichever is greater. Away from home base it is at least as long as the preceding duty, or 10 hours, whichever is greater, and must include an 8-hour sleep opportunity. A recurrent extended recovery rest of at least 36 hours, including two local nights, is due at least every 168 hours.

Cumulative limits

  • Duty: 60 hours in any 7 days, 110 hours in any 14 days, 190 hours in any 28 days.
  • Flight time: 100 hours in any 28 days, 900 hours in a calendar year, 1,000 hours in any 12 consecutive months.

These are the limits people miss most often. A single duty can be legal while the week around it is not.

Doing this for every duty

Working it out by hand for one duty is fine. Questions

Do UK CAA and SHGM use the same table?

The UK kept the EASA ORO.FTL structure after leaving the EU, and the Turkish SHT-FTL follows the same model. Operators can hold approved variations, so your airline's operations manual is the reference that applies to you.

What is the WOCL?

The window of circadian low: 02:00 to 05:59 in the time zone you are acclimatised to. Duties that touch it face tighter rules on extensions and count as disruptive schedules.

Does this cover FAA Part 117?

Not on this page. The Crewops app handles Part 117 as well.
