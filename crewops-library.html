The problem

Why the current revision is hard to be sure of

Revisions

Arrive as a PDF by email or a portal download. Crew members are often unsure which copy on the tablet or phone is current.

Changes

Are buried in a long file. The crew member has to find what changed before reading it.

Confirmation

That each crew member has read a revision is collected on paper or chased by email.

With Crewops Library

The phone holds the current revision only, says what changed, opens offline once downloaded and sends the confirmation back.

On the crew phone

What your crews see

Inside the crew app your crews already use for their roster, in seven languages. The Library sits on Me, under My airline.

Library on Me

The documents the airline publishes, searchable by title or code, with filters for new revisions, bookmarks and category.

One page per document

Code, title, revision, revision date, effective date, owner and authority approval as your office entered it at the top, then what changed in this revision.

Offline

One tap keeps the file in the app's private storage. Once it is downloaded, the manual opens on a layover with no signal. When a new revision is published, the old file is removed; a withdrawn document leaves the phone.

Read and confirm

When the revision asks for it, the crew member confirms with one tap, with the date. A confirmation made offline waits and goes out later.

Bookmarks

The documents a crew member opens most, kept at the top.

In the operator portal

What your department does

In its own panel of the operator portal. Your airline's operator code opens it, and a code can open this product alone. Nothing to install.

Publish

Code, title, type (manual, procedure, policy, bulletin, form, checklist, regulation, certificate), category, revision, revision and effective dates, change log, file link, an authority-approval field that you fill in, and a read-and-confirm with its due date.

New revision

One click opens the document with its code and title filled in. The phones pick up the revision the next time the app opens, and the older offline copy is removed.

Who has confirmed

Confirmations by crew code and date for every revision that asks for one, counted against the crew list when Academy records are loaded.

Withdraw

Takes a document off every phone the next time it goes online. It stays in the history in the portal.

At a glance

Documents live, revisions in the last 30 days, revisions awaiting confirmation, crew on record.

On the Today board

Revisions past their read-by date appear on the portal's Today board, counted against the crew list loaded with Academy records. Each line opens its document.

From your document system

Library works on its own and can be bought alone. When you want it connected, your document system publishes revisions through the API with a key made in the portal; every endpoint is in the API reference. A signed webhook tells your systems each time a crew member confirms a revision.

Regulatory basis

Built on the rules you work to

Revision control is what the manual rules already ask for. Library makes it visible on the phone. Crewops Library is built around those rules; your procedures and records prevail.

  • ORO.MLR.100: the operations manual: kept up to date, available to the people who need it, amendments made known
  • ORO.MLR.101: the structure of the operations manual, Parts A to D
  • ORO.GEN.200: communicating safety information to the people who need it
  • Authority approval: your documentation office fills in this field. The phone shows the mark as entered, and Crewops does not verify it

Questions

What departments ask first

Is it an EFB?

No. Crewops Library is the crew member's reference copy and the revision and confirmation trail. It is not an approved EFB application, and your EFB and its approvals stay as they are.

Where do the files live?

On your document server, or uploaded to Crewops, where the file sits on Cloudflare servers that Hawk & Mason Studios runs, with each airline's records kept apart by its airline code. The documentation office publishes a link with each revision or uploads the PDF, which is served only to your paired crews, and each phone downloads the file once into the app's private storage.

Can we see a sample?

Yes: a two-page demonstration manual, fictional and written for this page, is the file the demo library uses.

What do our crews need?

The crew app, your airline's company code and their own crew code. No account and no password. Crews of an airline that has not bought Crewops Library do not see the Library.
