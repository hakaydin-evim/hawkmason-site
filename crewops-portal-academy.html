Training & qualifications

Read-and-sign notices land on every crew phone and come back confirmed, one tap each. Qualification records (OPC, line check, SEP, DG, CRM, cabin recurrent…) are uploaded as a CSV and shown to each crew member as their own; the validity periods follow ORO.FC.230 / ORO.CC.140 as your department sets them. Courses are assigned with a due date and a link; completion comes back from the phone for your department to verify. Records are kept for the periods of ORO.MLR.115.
