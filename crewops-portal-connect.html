Crewops Aviation Platform · connections

Connect your own systems

A webhook sends each event to your system the moment it happens: a new safety report into your SMS, a completed course into your training records, a confirmed revision into your document system, a completed task into your own work tracking. Every delivery is signed (header x-crewops-signature: t=…,v1=…, HMAC-SHA256 of t.body with your secret). One try, 5 seconds; the last 20 deliveries are listed below. Your data also leaves as CSV from each product.

API KEYS · FOR YOUR OWN SYSTEMS

A key reads and writes what this operator code opens (same products, same expiry), for one system at a time: your SMS, HR or training system, your document system. Revoke a key without touching the others or your code. Every endpoint is described on the API reference.
