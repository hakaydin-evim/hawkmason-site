Controlled documents

Manuals, procedures, bulletins and forms on every crew phone, current revision only. Publish a document once under its code (OM-A, CCM…); each new revision replaces the one on the phones, keeps the history here and removes the old offline copy. Mark a revision "read and confirm" and see who has confirmed it. The file stays on your own file host (an https link); the app downloads it once for offline reading.
