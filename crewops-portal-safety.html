Crewops Safety · the operator's workspace

Safety office

Reports your crews send from the Crewops app, with the flight, aircraft, stations and duty times filled in from their roster. Classify, act and reply here; the reply goes back to the reporter's phone. The 72-hour clock is Regulation (EU) 376/2014 Art. 4(8): a reportable occurrence reaches the authority within 72 hours; mark a report "not reportable" to stop it. The E5X file is the ECCAIRS format: upload it to the ECCAIRS 2 reporting portal or your authority's ECCAIRS system and complete the operator, aircraft model and aerodrome there. The CSV follows the Annex I fields. Both are exports; the submission stays yours.

WEB REPORT LINK · FOR CREWS WITHOUT THE APP

Share it on your own channels (crew portal, e-mail, a QR code in the crew room). Reports sent through it arrive here like app reports, marked "web". A new link stops the old one.

The hazard and risk register (ICAO Doc 9859 5×5: probability 1–5 × severity A–E). Initial and residual risk, mitigations with owner and due date, the reports a risk came from, and a review date. Open a report and press ADD TO RISK REGISTER to start from it.

NEW RISK

Audits and inspections with their findings (level 1, level 2, observation per ORO.GEN.150): corrective action plan, owner, due date, closure with evidence.

NEW AUDIT OR INSPECTION

Management of change (ORO.GEN.200): what changes, the hazards it brings, the risks it links to in the register, the decision and the review after it is in place.

NEW CHANGE

Safety performance indicators from your own records: reports per month, by kind, time to close, replies, open and overdue actions, the risk picture and open findings.
