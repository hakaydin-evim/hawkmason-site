Questions

What departments ask first

Is Crewops Safety a complete SMS?

No. It keeps the safety records in one place. Your SMS manual, procedures and approvals stay yours.

Does it replace our reporting system?

No. It runs beside your SMS or feeds it. Your office makes any submission to the authority.

Who can read a report?

Your safety office, and the systems you choose to connect. Each airline's records are kept apart.
