What it does

Everything your roster does not tell you

📥

Reads the roster you already have

Import from your crew system, a pasted calendar link or any roster PDF. The app reads it on the device, from the text layer with OCR as the fallback. 273 operators are preset in the picker.

⏱️

Limits from the published tables

EASA tables, also applied for UK CAA and SHGM, and FAA Part 117. FDP ceilings, minimum rest, cumulative duty, and the earliest they can call you again — with the margin the roster leaves, not just the numbers on it.

🌙

Fatigue you can point at

An indicator built from the schedule itself: WOCL encroachment, early starts, short rest, consecutive days, time-zone displacement. Never a fitness-to-fly verdict. That call stays yours.

📗

A logbook you own

Sectors you confirm become logbook rows. EASA and FAA CSV export whenever you want them. Cabin crew get their own flight-and-duty record with block time, duty time and night stops.

🩺

Coaching, with sources

Sleep, body clock, light and caffeine windows, cosmic radiation against the Euratom reference levels. Seventy-six cards that fire off your own numbers — 27 on fatigue, 22 on sleep, 15 on the body clock, 12 on radiation — each naming the study or regulation behind it.

🗂️

Documents that warn you early

Licence, medical, passport and visa expiry dates, with reminders before they bite, and a note of which destinations need which visa.

☁️

Your devices, in step

Optional iCloud sync keeps your roster, logbook, documents and settings the same on every device you own, through your own iCloud account. Off by default, encrypted on the device with a key that never leaves your devices. Sleep, fatigue and IMSAFE entries never go to iCloud.

📅

Every change, on record

A roster moves several times a week. Crewops keeps every update: what changed, day by day, with the old time struck through beside the new one, the duty that was added and the one that was taken away. Last Monday's change is still there on Friday.

🗺️

Every sector, mapped

The routes you have flown drawn on one map, with block hours, sectors and airports adding up underneath — flown sectors only, deadheads left out, the hours still planned shown apart. Every duty shows its own route too. After each flight, a summary — block, FDP margin, fatigue, estimated dose — that you confirm into the logbook and can share as an image.

📝

Notes that stay

A note on every duty and on every airport: the gate that always changes, the hotel shuttle, the crew room code. Refreshing the roster never wipes them, and they travel with iCloud sync when it is on.

🧑‍✈️

Your crew, one screen

Crewmates shares your roster, read-only, with the colleagues you invite, and shows you theirs: who is flying, who is off, and a grid of the weeks ahead — press and hold a day to see that flight. The share panel names the people it is shared with, invitations still unanswered marked. It runs through your own iCloud with Apple's sharing, costs nothing, works offline on the last copy, and never includes sleep, fatigue or health entries. Stop sharing or leave whenever you like.

🔒

On your Lock Screen, in your calendar

Home and Lock Screen widgets in your colour theme show the next duty, and a Live Activity counts down to report on the Dynamic Island — and on to release if you want it through the duty. The roster can mirror into your calendar with a reminder before each report. All local: no server, no push.

Your roster, your hours and your health are on your device, not on our server. We do not have an account system, because we do not want your data.

Crewops computes everything on the device. The only things it fetches or sends are public weather and space-weather feeds, your own airline's portal or calendar link when you ask it to, signed app updates from our own server, your Pro subscription status if you subscribe, and, only if you switch sharing on, your post-flight rating, sent for your airline to read. If you switch iCloud sync on, an encrypted copy goes to your own iCloud account and nowhere else. Crewmates shares only your duties, only with the people you invite, through your iCloud.

Works with your crew system

One app, whichever system rosters you

Signs in and refreshes

NetLine/CrewLink signs in to your airline's CrewLink portal and refreshes the roster on its own, change notices included. JetXBit signs in with your portal user name, takes the emailed confirmation code once, then refreshes on its own. It is verified with crew in October 2026.

Page, PDF or calendar link

AIMS eCrew, CrewConnex, FLiCA, Leon, CAE Crew Access, RAIDO and Sabre CWP are read from the roster page opened in the app, its PDF or a calendar link, depending on the system. A pasted calendar link refreshes by itself. Any .ics calendar or roster PDF, text or scanned, is read on the phone.

Questions

What crew ask first

Which crew rostering systems does Crewops read?

NetLine/CrewLink and JetXBit sign in and refresh the roster on their own. AIMS eCrew, CrewConnex, FLiCA, Leon, CAE Crew Access, RAIDO and Sabre CWP are read from their roster pages, PDFs or calendar links. Any .ics calendar or roster PDF works too. If yours is not read yet, the app sends us a sample in one tap and we add it.

Which flight time limitation rules does it use?

EASA ORO.FTL tables, also applied for UK CAA and SHGM, and FAA Part 117. They give the flight duty period ceiling, minimum rest, cumulative duty and the margin the roster leaves you.

Is my roster uploaded, and does my airline see my data?

No. There is no account and no Crewops server holding your roster; everything is computed on the phone. Optional iCloud sync and Crewmates go through your own iCloud account. Your airline sees nothing from the app, unless you switch on sharing a post-flight rating with it. The privacy policy has the details.

Does Crewops work for cabin crew?

Yes. Cabin crew get the same duty limits, rest and fatigue views, and their own flight-and-duty record with block time, duty time and night stops.

Can I export my logbook?

Yes. Flown sectors become logbook rows you can export as EASA or FAA CSV at any time. Deadhead sectors count as duty, never as flight time.

What does Crewops cost?

Crewops is free to use. Crewops Pro is an optional subscription with a one-month free trial. The App Store shows the current price.

Questions, bugs, or a rule set we got wrong
