Questions

What crew ask first

Which crew rostering systems does Crewops read?

NetLine/CrewLink and JetXBit refresh on their own; AIMS eCrew, CrewConnex, FLiCA, Leon, CAE Crew Access, RAIDO and Sabre CWP are read from their roster pages, PDFs or calendar links. Other .ics calendars and roster PDFs work when their layout is known.

Is my roster uploaded, and does my airline see my data?

No. There is no account and no Crewops server holding your roster; everything is computed on the phone. Your airline sees nothing from the app unless you share a post-flight rating, or file or sign something in a product it licenses. Details in the privacy policy.

Which flight time limitation rules does it use?

EASA ORO.FTL tables, also applied for UK CAA and SHGM, and FAA Part 117, give the flight duty period ceiling, minimum rest, cumulative duty and the margin your roster leaves you.

Does Crewops work for cabin crew?

Yes. Cabin crew get the same duty limits, rest and fatigue views, plus a flight-and-duty record with block time, duty time and night stops.

Can I export my logbook?

Yes, as EASA or FAA CSV at any time. Deadhead sectors count as duty, never as flight time.

What does Crewops cost?

Free to use. Crewops Pro adds the limit board, Rhythm, Health, the full coaching library, post-flight summaries and automatic roster refresh, with a one-month free trial; the App Store shows the price.
