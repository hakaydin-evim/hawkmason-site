Pondr

Pondr: 15 minutes

An app for thinking: one subject you chose, 15 minutes a day with the question on the screen, a few lines of writing, and then a mirror — a short note and one question written by an AI model, in your name, with no advice and no praise. Not a chatbot, not therapy. No streaks, no scores, no account; your writing stays on your phone unless you ask for the mirror.

Sugar Tiles

Sugar Tiles: Block Puzzle

Drop three candy blocks on an 8×8 board, clear rows and columns, and watch them burst into sparkles. A 768-level journey through 64 hand-crafted worlds, an endless Classic mode, a fresh Daily Treat and Weekly Box challenges. No timers, a day and night look, and it plays offline — two calm minutes or a long cozy session, your call.

Ittako

Ittako: Toy Night Climb

A small toy fell off the bed and has all night to climb back. One finger: grip a spinning arm and let go at the right moment, hold to wind a propeller and glide. Five layers of one room — the carpet forest, the desk, the shelves, the curtain, the ceiling stars — each with its own light, and a night that keeps going above the bed. One-time purchase, no ads, no tracking, quiet enough for a bedroom.

Wirelit

Wirelit: Turn the rings, light the city

The city’s power is out and you are the night watchman. Tap a piece and its whole ring turns; turn the rings until current reaches every window lamp, and a window in the skyline lights up for good. Twelve neighbourhoods, each with its own light and musical key, and a new box every night, the same for everyone. No score, no timer, no ads. Coming in 2027 to iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch.

Emlak Kralı

Emlak Kralı: İstanbul

Start with the flat your grandmother left you in Moda and become Kadıköy’s biggest landlord. Every building on the map is a real one, drawn from OpenStreetMap: buy flats one by one, renovate tired blocks, take over whole buildings and their rooftop billboards, and win five chapters before Mehpare Yapı and two other rivals get there first. A Weekly Race on Game Center; no energy, no pop-up ads, no paid power. Coming soon to iPhone.

The studio

Hawk & Mason Studios

We’re an independent studio with a simple belief: the best mobile products are calm, colorful and respect your time. We design in-engine, test on real devices, and ship when it feels right.

Founder

Hawk & Mason Studios is run by Hakan Aydın. Hakan has spent more than twelve years in airline operations and has taught CRM and fatigue risk management to crews.

Crewops came straight out of that work: the duty limits, rest rules and fatigue he knows from the inside, put in a crew member’s pocket.

Everything else comes from a love of games and of small things done well. Sugar Tiles grew into a 768-level journey across 64 candy worlds. Pondr began as a fifteen-minute thinking habit, and its little ring puzzle grew into Wirelit, a game now being built for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch. Based in Antalya, Türkiye.

Our door is open. If your team has a problem worth solving — an app, a tool, a workflow that needs to work — we can sit with you, build it and ship it.
