How to move iPhone photos to a Windows PC, sorted by date

Three ways to do it, and what your folder looks like afterwards.

Getting photos off an iPhone and onto a Windows PC is easy. Finding one of them a year later is the hard part: the files arrive with names like IMG_4821 in folders that say nothing about when anything was taken. This page goes through the two methods built into Windows and then ours, which is made for exactly this.

1. File Explorer and a cable

Plug the iPhone in, unlock it, tap Trust, and it appears in File Explorer under This PC. Inside Internal Storage there is a DCIM folder you can copy from.

  • Good: nothing to install, and you get the original files.
  • Less good: the folders are named by the phone, not by you, and edited photos, Live Photo videos and screenshots sit mixed together. Sorting it out is manual work.

2. The Windows Photos app

With the iPhone connected by cable, open Photos on the PC and choose Import. You can pick which items to bring in and where to put them.

  • Good: built in, and it remembers what was already imported.
  • Less good: it needs the cable every time, large libraries can be slow to list, and what is already on your PC from earlier years stays as messy as it was.

3. Sortwell, over Wi-Fi, into year and month folders

Sortwell is an iPhone app with a free Windows companion. Photos go from the phone to the PC over your own Wi-Fi and are filed by the date they were taken: 

Photos\
  2023\
    2023-06\
    2023-07\
  2024\
    2024-01\
  1. Install Sortwell for Windows and choose your photo folder.
  2. Click Connect iPhone. A QR code appears.
  3. In Sortwell on the iPhone, choose Send photos to your PC and scan the code.
  4. Mark photos in any review and choose Send to PC.
  • Good: no cable, no iTunes, no iCloud and no account. Originals are sent, including the video half of Live Photos. The same app can sort the folders you already have on the PC into the same year and month layout, and undo it.
  • Less good: both devices have to be on the same Wi-Fi, and sending and sorting are part of Sortwell Pro, which is bought in the iPhone app. Looking through a folder by date on the PC is free.

Tidying photos that are already on the PC

Open the folder in Sortwell for Windows. It reads the date inside each photo and video, not the date the file was copied, and shows everything by year and month without moving a file. If you like what you see, Sort into date folders moves them into that layout; Undo last sort puts every file back where it was. Nothing is deleted or overwritten.

Then free up the iPhone

Once the photos are on the PC, Sortwell on the iPhone finds exact duplicates, similar shots, large videos and screenshots, and removes what you mark in one confirmed step. iOS keeps deleted items for 30 days in case you change your mind.
