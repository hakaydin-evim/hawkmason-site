Ittako — Privacy Policy

Effective date: __YURURLUK__

Ittako is free to play and shows ads. The game itself does not ask who you are: there is no account, no sign-up and no analytics of our own, and your save file stays on your device. The ads come from Google AdMob, and Google’s advertising software inside the app collects some device and usage information to show and measure them — listed below. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time in Settings → Remove ads → Privacy choices. A one-time purchase removes the ads between runs. On iPhone and iPad, Apple’s Game Center is optional.

Hawk & Mason Studios is a trading name of Hakan Aydın, an independent developer based in Türkiye. Contact: hello@hawkmason.com.

What the app stores

The game writes one file on your device, inside the app’s own private storage (save.json, plus a backup copy). It contains only game state:

  • your best height, per-layer best heights, and daily-run results;
  • totals: runs played, falls, metres climbed;
  • a short log of your last 40 runs (time, height, falls, whether a shortcut was used, whether the bed was reached);
  • which cosmetics you have unlocked and which are selected;
  • your goal level and which goals you have completed;
  • your settings (sound, haptics, reduced shake, accessibility options);
  • which tutorial hints you have already seen, and your night streak;
  • whether you own “Remove ads”.

This file never leaves your device. It contains no name, no email, no device ID, no advertising ID and no location. Deleting the app deletes it; Settings → erase save resets your progress (a purchase is restored from the store).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads appear only between runs, and when you choose to watch one to continue after a fall. They are delivered by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK, which is built into the app and, as Google describes it, collects and processes:

  • Device identifiers — such as the advertising identifier or app- or developer-bounded identifiers — for advertising and analytics;
  • Advertising data — such as the ads you have seen;
  • Product interactions — such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • Coarse location — derived from your IP address;
  • Performance and diagnostic data — such as launch time, hang rate and crash logs.

Google uses this information as set out in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We do not receive any of it about you individually — only aggregate reports (impressions, earnings).

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and wherever the law requires it, Google’s consent form asks you before personalised ads are shown. Change your choice any time in Settings → Remove ads → Privacy choices.
  • iPhone and iPad. The app does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so it does not access your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Android. Google may use the device’s advertising ID; you can reset or delete it in your device settings.
  • Content. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Purchases

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); their own privacy policies apply to it. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether you own the item, so it can turn the ads off and restore the purchase on a new device.

Game Center (optional, iPhone and iPad)

Game Center is Apple’s service, not ours. Signing in, your Game Center nickname and the scores held on the leaderboards are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you are signed in, the game sends one integer — your height in whole metres — when a run ends. Your save file, settings and run log are never sent. To stop sending, sign out in your device’s Settings > Game Center. There is no Game Center on Android.

What the app does not do

  • No account and no analytics of our own. We run no server for this game and receive nothing from it. The game plays fully offline; ads simply do not appear without a connection.
  • No selling of personal data. We do not sell or share your information; advertising data goes to Google as described above.

Required-reason APIs

Apple requires a declared reason for certain system APIs. The game declares three categories and uses them only to read and write its own files and to keep time: File timestamp (DDA9.1, C617.1), System boot time (35F9.1) and Disk space (E174.1, 85F4.1). Google’s SDK carries its own privacy manifest.

Children

Ittako is rated for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and it shows ads. Ads are restricted to the most general content rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us.
