Three things to learn. That’s the whole game.

🌀

Don’t jump. Get slung.

Grip a spinning arm and let go at the right moment. The arm throws you the way it is travelling, not the way it is pointing — so the strongest launch is not where you expect it. Finding it is the whole game.

🪁

Hold to glide.

A propeller winds up while you hold. Use it to stretch a jump, to slow a fall, or to save a jump you misjudged.

🌙

Quiet enough for a bedroom.

Tap, hold, drag. Portrait, one hand, playable on a bus — and calm enough for a room where someone is already asleep.

One room

Five worlds, stacked on top of each other

A child’s bedroom is enormous when you are the size of a toy. Every layer has its own light — a nightlight, a desk lamp, an aquarium, the moon, the plastic stars — and every layer you reach stays lit in your room, permanently.

And above the ceiling — where the room runs out — the Star Floor keeps going.

Three ways to climb

A mode for every kind of night

The climb

Reach the bed and the night is yours. Come back and the bed becomes a doorway — above it, the Star Floor goes on as far as you can.

Daily

The same room for everyone, once a day. The night is generated from the date, so the level you climb is the level everyone else climbs.

Dream

No falling. No counter. No record. Just the room. Some nights you don’t want to be measured.

Honest by design

Ittako is a game you buy once. It has no way to take anything else from you — not your money, not your attention, not your data.

  • 0ads
  • 0in-app purchases
  • 0accounts
  • 0network requests

The last one is literal: the game contains no networking code at all, so it works in airplane mode and collects nothing. Progress lives in one file on your device.
Accessibility options — longer glide, softer gravity, reduced shake — are settings, not rewards. Nothing is locked behind them, and runs made with them are quietly kept in a separate record. Read the privacy policy.

Four verbs. That’s the control scheme.

  • Tap — jump
  • Tap on a spinning arm — let go and get slung
  • Hold — the propeller winds up, you glide
  • Drag while holding — steer the glide
