Merhaba — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Merhaba is an app for learning to speak Turkish. It has no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your progress is kept on your iPhone and is not sent to us. When you practice speaking, your voice is turned into text by Apple’s speech recognition so the app can check what you said; we never receive the audio or the text.

Hawk & Mason Studios is a trading name of Hakan Aydın, an independent developer based in Türkiye. Contact: hello@hawkmason.com.

Your voice

Speaking exercises ask for the microphone and for speech recognition. The microphone is on only while you are recording an answer: from the moment you tap the microphone button until you stop or go quiet.

The recording is turned into text by Apple’s speech recognition service. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process the audio on its servers to do this; Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to that processing. Merhaba compares the resulting text with the phrase you were asked to say, shows you the result, and then discards both. Recordings and transcripts are not saved in the app and are not sent to us.

Turkish phrases are read aloud by your iPhone’s built-in voice. That happens on the device.

What the app stores

Merhaba keeps the following in its own private storage on your iPhone: the answers you gave when you first opened it (your level, why you are learning, the topics you picked and your daily goal), the lessons you finished, your points and streak, the days you practiced, the phrases you saved, and your settings. None of this leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

What it downloads

When it opens, Merhaba checks hawkmason.com for an updated set of lessons and downloads it if there is one. This is an ordinary web request: it carries no identifier, no account and nothing about your progress. As with any web request, the server that answers it sees the IP address the request came from. We do not use it to identify you.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the app schedules them on your iPhone at the time you choose. They are local notifications: nothing is sent from a server, and we do not receive a device token. You can turn them off in the app or in Settings.

Purchases

Merhaba Pro is a subscription processed by Apple; Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to it. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether your subscription is active, so it can open the paid lessons and restore the purchase on a new device.

What the app does not do

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No ads and no tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics software and does not ask for permission to track you.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data. We have none to sell or share.

Required-reason APIs

Apple requires a declared reason for certain system APIs. Merhaba declares one: User defaults (CA92.1), used only to remember its own settings and your progress.

Children

Merhaba is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@hawkmason.com

If this policy ever changes, the effective date above changes with it.
