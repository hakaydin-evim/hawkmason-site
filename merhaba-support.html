Frequently asked

The app cannot hear me.

Open Settings, find Merhaba, and check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are both on. Speech recognition for Turkish needs an internet connection on most devices.

I said it right but it was marked wrong.

The check is done by Apple’s speech recognition, which sometimes mishears, especially in a noisy room or with a short word. Tap the microphone and try again, a little slower. After two tries you can tap Move on. Listening with Slowly first often helps.

It stops listening before I finish.

Recording ends after a short silence. If you need more time, keep speaking without a long pause, or tap the microphone again to retry.

The Turkish voice sounds flat.

Phrases are read by your iPhone’s built-in Turkish voice. You can download a higher-quality one in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > Turkish.

What is free and what is Pro?

Ten lessons are free: the sounds unit and the first unit in full, plus a taster lesson from three other units, with two conversations. Merhaba Pro opens every lesson, conversation and card deck. It is a monthly or yearly subscription.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Merhaba. Subscriptions are billed by Apple and renew until cancelled there. Cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial ends to avoid being charged.

I bought Pro but lessons are still locked.

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore in the top corner. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do reminders work?

Turn on Daily reminder in Profile > Settings and pick a time. On a day you have already practiced, the reminder is skipped. Streak alerts adds an evening warning when a streak is about to end.

I lost my streak.

A streak grows when you finish a lesson or conversation on consecutive days. If you miss one day, a streak freeze covers it automatically while you have freezes left.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Progress is kept on your device and travels with an iPhone backup or transfer. There is no account, so it is not synced separately.

Reporting a problem

The most useful report has three lines:

  • what you were doing, and which lesson or conversation you were in;
  • what you expected and what happened instead;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version.

Send it to hello@hawkmason.com.
