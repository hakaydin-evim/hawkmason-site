Merhaba: Learn Turkish

For iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

Merhaba gets you speaking Turkish from the first lesson. You hear a phrase, say it out loud, and see which words came out right. Tako, a small wind-up toy with a propeller, keeps you company along the way.

What you do in the app

  • Speak. Every lesson asks you to say the phrase, then marks each word you got right.
  • Learn the sounds first. A starter unit covers the six Turkish letters English does not have.
  • Hold a conversation. Order tea, ride a dolmuş, check in to a hotel or haggle at the bazaar with Tako playing the other side.
  • Pick up the grammar as you go. Short notes before a lesson explain one idea at a time, such as endings and vowel harmony.
  • Speak like the dramas. Whole units cover the Turkish you hear in series: love, arguments, palace intrigue and tough talk.
  • Review. Flashcards, verb decks for past, present and future, and a quick review of what you practiced least.

Free and Pro

The sounds unit, the first unit and the first lesson of every other unit are free, along with two conversations. Merhaba Pro opens everything else. It is an optional subscription, monthly or yearly, and the price is shown in the app before you buy.

Your privacy

There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your progress stays on your iPhone. The details are in the privacy policy.
