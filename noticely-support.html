Frequently asked

Which iPhones work?

Noticely needs iOS 27 and Apple Intelligence, which means iPhone 15 Pro or any later iPhone, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. On other iPhones the app runs in simple mode: text recognition only, so it reads less and asks you to review more.

How does it read my receipts?

Pick a photo with the + button, snap one with the camera, or share an image from any app. Noticely also looks at new photos in your library on its own: a few at launch, more at night while charging. Only the document types you chose during setup are read. A record appears on the Today screen; tap it to check or correct the fields.

What can I ask Siri?

  • "When does the Dyson warranty end?"
  • "Noticely, what's coming up?"
  • "Noticely, what did I spend this week?"
  • "Noticely, add the electricity bill to my calendar"

Siri only knows the records you approved. If a record is waiting for review, approve it first with Correct, teach Siri.

A record came out wrong.

Open it and edit any field, or change its type with the Type picker. The app remembers the store or sender you corrected and classifies it right next time. Deleting a record also removes it from Siri and cancels its reminders.

Reminders

Approved records set their own reminders: thirty days before a warranty ends, three days before and on a due date, fourteen days before a medication expires, the evening before and two hours before an appointment, on delivery day. Today's delivery, appointment or due payment also appears on the Lock Screen.

What is in Pro?

Reading, Siri, reminders and the widget are free. Pro adds unlimited history (free lists the last 90 days), a back-scan of your whole photo library (free reads only photos taken after you installed the app) and the weekly digest. Yearly, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Restoring purchases

Settings → Pro → Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they come back on a new iPhone.

Does anything leave my phone?

No. Reading happens on the device, we have no server, there is no account. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete everything. Records, the Siri index, reminders and Lock Screen cards are cleared. Your photos are not touched.
