Snap it. Forget it. Ask Siri.

Your photo library is full of "I'll look at it later": receipts, warranty cards, medicine boxes, shipping screens, bills, appointment messages. Noticely reads them on your iPhone and teaches Siri, so you can ask "when does the Dyson warranty end?" without opening anything.

Four thousand photos. Three hundred of them are promises.

How it works

Nothing to type. Nothing to file.

📷

Snap or screenshot

Take a photo, grab a screenshot, or share an image from any app. Noticely also looks at new photos on its own, a few at launch and more at night while charging. Only the document types you choose.

✨

Read on your iPhone

Apple Intelligence reads the document on the device: store, date, amount, warranty, due date, tracking number, dose. A record appears; you can correct any field, and the app learns the correction.

🗣️

Ask Siri

"What's coming up?" "What did I spend this week?" "Add the electricity bill to my calendar." Siri answers from what Noticely taught it. The app never opens.

What it notices

Six kinds of paper, one quiet assistant

Receipts & warranties

Reminds you thirty days before a warranty ends.

Bills

Three days before and on the due date. Today's bill sits on your Lock Screen.

Deliveries

Tracking number and delivery day, with a Lock Screen card on the day.

Appointments

Matched with your calendar; a reminder the evening before and two hours before.

Medicine boxes

Dose and expiry, with a reminder two weeks before it runs out.

The week

Money picture and upcoming items on one screen; a widget on your Home Screen.

Privacy

Never leaves your phone. We have no server.

Reading happens on the device with Apple's on-device models. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising. Family and holiday photos are never read, and there is no face recognition. Card numbers, passwords and ID numbers are deliberately left out of every record.
