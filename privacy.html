Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026

Hawk & Mason builds mobile games with a simple privacy promise: collect only what is needed to operate, improve, protect, and support the game.

Information we may collect

Our games may process gameplay progress, device diagnostics, crash logs, approximate region, purchase status, advertising consent signals, and support messages you choose to send.

How we use information

  • To save progress and keep game features working.
  • To understand crashes, bugs, performance, and balancing issues.
  • To process purchases, restore entitlements, and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to support, press, and partnership messages.
  • To show ads only where allowed by platform rules and consent choices.

Third-party services

Hawk & Mason games may use platform services from Apple, Google, payment processors, analytics, crash reporting, and advertising providers. These services process data under their own policies and platform requirements.

Children

Hawk & Mason does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is permitted by platform services and applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

Your choices

You can manage app permissions, ad tracking choices, subscriptions, and purchases through your device, App Store, or Google Play account settings.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@hawkmason.com
