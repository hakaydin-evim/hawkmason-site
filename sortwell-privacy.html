Sortwell — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Sortwell is a photo cleaner for iPhone. Everything it does with your photos and videos happens on your iPhone. Nothing from your library is uploaded to us or to anyone else: we run no server for this app, and the app has no account, no ads and no analytics.

Your photo library

Sortwell asks for access to your photo library so it can find items you may want to remove: exact duplicates, similar shots, large videos and screenshots. The scan uses Apple’s on-device image analysis. Your photos and videos are read on the iPhone and never leave it through Sortwell.

Nothing is deleted until you confirm, and iOS shows its own confirmation as well. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple removes them from your other devices too; that sync is Apple’s, not ours.

What the app stores

So that it can open without scanning again, Sortwell keeps the result of the last scan in its own private storage on your device: for each item, the identifier iOS gives it, its date, its size and its type, and which items were grouped together. It also keeps a count of how many free deletions you have used. None of this leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

The Ask field

When you type a request such as “videos over 100 MB from 2023”, the sentence is read on your iPhone. On devices with Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple’s on-device language model reads it; on other devices the app reads it by keywords. Only the sentence you typed is read, not your photos, and it is not stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Sortwell Pro is sold as an in-app purchase processed by Apple; Apple’s Privacy Policy applies to it. We never see your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether you own Pro, so it can remove the free limit and restore the purchase on a new device.

What the app does not do

  • No account and no server. There is nothing to sign up for and nowhere for your data to go.
  • No ads and no tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics software and does not ask for permission to track you.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data. We have none to sell or share.

Required-reason APIs

Apple requires a declared reason for certain system APIs. Sortwell declares one: User defaults (CA92.1), used only to remember its own settings and the free-deletion count.

Children

Sortwell is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
