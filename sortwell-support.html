Frequently asked

Does Sortwell upload my photos?

No. The scan runs on your iPhone. Photos leave it only when you send them to your own PC, and they go straight there over your Wi-Fi, not through us. See the privacy policy.

I deleted something by mistake.

Open the Photos app, go to Recently Deleted, select the item and tap Recover. iOS keeps deleted items there for 30 days.

I deleted a lot, but my storage did not change.

Deleted items still take up space while they sit in Recently Deleted. Empty it in the Photos app to get the space back straight away.

How does it choose which similar shot to keep?

Photos taken a few seconds apart that look alike are grouped, and the one Apple’s on-device analysis scores highest is kept. Tap the card, or a thumbnail in the strip under it, to look at the others before you decide. You always have the last word: swipe right to keep an item.

The sizes look larger than what Settings shows.

Sortwell shows the size of the original file. If Optimize iPhone Storage is on, your iPhone may hold a smaller copy and keep the original in iCloud, so the space freed on the device can be less than the number shown.

What is free and what is Pro?

Scanning and looking through the results are free, and you can delete up to 50 items without paying. Sortwell Pro removes that limit. Pro is offered as a weekly or yearly subscription or as a one-time purchase.

How do I send photos to my PC?

Install Sortwell for Windows, open it and choose the folder where you keep your photos. Click Connect iPhone. On your iPhone open Sortwell, tap Send photos to your PC and point the camera at the code. After that, mark items in any review and tap Send to PC first; they arrive in folders by year and month. Deleting them from the iPhone is a separate step you confirm.

My iPhone cannot reach the PC.

Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network and Sortwell must be open on the PC. If Windows asked whether to allow Sortwell on the network, it needs to be allowed on private networks. On the iPhone, check that Local Network is on for Sortwell in Settings. If your PC’s network address changed, scan the code again.

What is free on Windows?

Sortwell for Windows is free to download. Browsing a folder by year and month is free. Sorting files into date folders and receiving photos from the iPhone are part of Sortwell Pro, which you buy in the iPhone app; scanning the code from an iPhone with Pro unlocks them on the PC. With a subscription, connect the iPhone again after it renews.

Windows warns me about an unknown publisher.

The installer is not yet signed with a publisher certificate, so Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Only download it from hawkmason.com.

I sorted a folder on Windows and want it back the way it was.

Click Undo last sort. Every file goes back to the folder it came from. Sorting never deletes or overwrites a file.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Sortwell. Subscriptions are billed by Apple and renew until cancelled there.

I bought Pro but the app still shows the limit.

Open Sortwell, tap the gear, then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Reporting a problem

The most useful report has three lines:

  • what you were doing;
  • roughly how many photos and videos are in your library;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version.

Send it to hello@hawkmason.com.
