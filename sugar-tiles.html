Questions

Sugar Tiles block puzzle FAQ

What kind of game is Sugar Tiles?

Sugar Tiles is a block puzzle game. You drop candy blocks onto an 8×8 grid and clear full rows and columns. It mixes a calm, endless block-blasting mode with a 768-level journey full of goals and blockers.

Is Sugar Tiles free?

Yes. Sugar Tiles is free to download and play on iPhone, iPad and Android. The game is supported by ads, and the optional Sugar Club subscription removes banner and interstitial ads and adds daily helpers.

Can I play Sugar Tiles offline?

Yes. The block puzzle, the Journey levels and Classic mode all work without Wi-Fi or mobile data, so you can play on a plane or the metro.

How many levels are there?

There are 768 Journey levels across 64 candy worlds, plus a fresh Daily Treat puzzle every day and a Weekly Event with its own goals and rewards.

What are the special blocks?

Four blockers keep the puzzle fresh: chocolate breaks with one line, ice and crates need two lines, and jam spills onto cells that you can still place blocks on.
