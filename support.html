Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the app and use the in-game restore option (in Sugar Tiles, on the Sugar Club screen). If your App Store or Google Play account was previously charged, your entitlement will be restored.

How can I cancel a subscription?

Open your iPhone's Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions, tap the active app (for example Sugar Tiles), and select “Cancel Subscription”.

How do I delete my account and data?

In Sugar Tiles, open Settings → “Delete account & data”. This removes your anonymous player record and its cloud save and clears local progress on the device. For any other app, email us at hello@hawkmason.com and we will action the request.
