Support

For game support, press, partnerships, or store review questions, contact Hawk & Mason by email.

Email: hello@hawkmason.com

Helpful details

If you are contacting support about Sugar Tiles, include your device, platform, game version, country or region, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful when a visual problem is involved.

Purchases

App Store and Google Play purchases are managed by their platform accounts. For restore or entitlement issues, include the store, product name, and approximate purchase date. Do not send full card details.
