A calm circuit puzzle

Wirelit

Turn the rings. Light the city.

The city’s power is out and you hold the keys to every fuse box: you are the night watchman. Every box is a small board of wires around a transformer. Tap a piece and its whole ring turns a quarter turn.

Turn the rings until current reaches every window lamp. When the last one catches, the box says “All the lamps are lit”, your phone gives one long, warm pulse, and a window in the skyline lights up for good.

How a box works

One rule, a dozen ideas built on it. No score, no timer, no move counter: only light.

↻

Tap to turn a ring

Any piece turns its whole ring a quarter turn. While your finger is down, the ring that will turn lights up, with an arrow for the way it goes.

⚡

Light every window

Current flows from the transformer at the centre. Bring it to every lamp; a sleeping window, drawn dashed, must stay dark.

◎

One answer, never one tap away

Every box has exactly one solution and none starts a single turn from solved. Stuck? The hint says a little more each time.

12 neighbourhoods, 288 boxes

Each neighbourhood has its own light, lamp shape, skyline and musical key, and brings one new idea: sleeping windows, chain and gear rings, bolted pieces, half and reverse rings, 9×9 squares.

Tonight’s box, everywhere

A new box every night, the same for everyone. Lit windows follow you through iCloud.

iPhone & iPad
Play tonight’s box right on the Home Screen with the large widget, or ask Siri how it is going. Unfold iPhone Duo for two pages: the box on one, the city on the other.
Mac
Click a piece, or turn rings with the number keys; the ring under the pointer lights up first.
Apple TV
The whole skyline on the big screen. Swipe to pick a ring, press to turn it.
Apple Watch
Every 5×5 box on your wrist, with the Digital Crown, and tonight’s box on a complication.
Game controller
The d-pad picks a ring, A turns it, Y asks for a hint.

Quiet by design

Nothing to chase
No score, no timer, no move counter, no leaderboards. Game Center achievements for each neighbourhood, the nightly boxes and the whole city.
Nothing about you
No ads, no account, no tracking, and no internet needed to play. Details in the privacy policy.
Sound and touch
Rings sing on a musical scale and a melody rises as lamps light; you feel every turn through haptics.
For everyone
Every ring can be turned with VoiceOver, text grows with Dynamic Type, and Reduce Motion stills the flowing current.
